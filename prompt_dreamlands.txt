I want to create a 3D Pygame living card video game (using moderngl) based on the works of H.P. Lovecraft. The cards will "come to life" and you'll be able to control them in real-time. His works are in the public domain where I live (the United States). The story will be generated/tweaked with machine learning/AI (HuggingFace transformers/sentence-transformers) by training off of his works (C:\Users\pswan\Documents\Books\Lovecraft-Complete-Fiction-Print.pdf). I will then come up with mechanics (inspired by other card games such as Yu-Gi-Oh!/Magic: The Gathering/Slay the Spire/Arkham Horror/Call of Cthulhu CCG/Mythos and other Lovecraftian games such as Bloodborne/Darkest Dungeon) with the help of AI. The ultimate goal is to make a product available on itch.io for $5. Open source fantasy trope 3d models from itch.io will be used until I can find a 3d model designer. The CPU card real-time AI will utilize reinforcement learning (Gambit/stable-baselines3/scikit-learn/tensorflow/pytorch). The cards themselves will be in Lua, with Lupa (https://github.com/scoder/lupa) being used. It will also have modding tools using Tkinter (including TkinterWeb)/PyWebView/NiceGUI. Tests will be conducted using pytest. The game engine will be in Cython. I will use pyinstaller to distribute an exe with the moddable game files. The game will be called Dream-Quest.
