# Story V2: Ideas

A smaller, tighter version of the story that **roughly follows _The Dream-Quest of Unknown Kadath_**. It folds in the parts of the current [STORY.md](STORY.md) that still fit.

This is a brainstorm, not a decision document. Every heading holds options. Keep the ones you like, and cross out the rest.

---

## 1. What changes from V1

| V1 (current STORY.md) | V2 |
|---|---|
| Portals tear open in the waking world; the player learns to seal them | **No portals.** The enemy invades the old way: fleets, sieges, raids, spies, occupation |
| Monsters pour into Arkham, Dunwich and Kingsport | The war is fought **mostly in the Dreamlands**, along the road Carter travels in the book |
| A large waking-world map, plus Innsmouth, the Mountains of Madness, Yith and Mi-Go as later content | The waking world shrinks to **dark-ages Arkham**: prologue, interludes and epilogue. The other waking-world horrors are cut or become post-game hooks |
| The Archsage arrives at the shop | The player **seeks out** the Archsage at a **college library**, early on |
| The sealing sign is the core mechanic | **Dreaming** is the core mechanic: going down the steps, waking, lucid tricks. Magic is learned at the college |
| Plot: close the gates, reach Kadath | Plot: **find the sunset city, reach Kadath and bring back the gods**, while an invasion from the sky cities spreads across the Dreamlands |

**Why this is smaller:**
- One main exterior landmass (the Dreamlands) and one small town (Arkham), instead of a whole valley plus a second world.
- One story that already exists and is tested, instead of two parts stitched together.
- Plain battles and sieges are easier to script than a portal system.

**Copyright note:** *Dream-Quest* was written in 1926–27 and published in 1943. It falls in the same "widely treated as public domain" group as the 1930s works in THE_DREAMLANDS.md §14.2. Add it to that table.

## 2. The book in one paragraph (the spine to follow)

1. Carter dreams three times of a marvellous **sunset city** and is snatched away each time, so he resolves to find it.
2. He goes down the **seventy steps** to the **Cavern of Flame** and the priests **Nasht and Kaman-Thah**, then down the **seven hundred steps** into the **Enchanted Wood**.
3. He consults the **zoogs**, then old **Atal** in **Ulthar**.
4. In **Dylath-Leen** he learns of the merchant with the ruby. He is **drugged onto a black galley** and taken **to the Moon**, where the **cats** rescue him.
5. He sails to **Oriab**, climbs **Ngranek** to see the gods' carved face, and is carried off by **night-gaunts** into the **Vale of Pnath**.
6. **Pickman's ghouls** guide him through the **Underworld**, past the **gugs** and **ghasts**.
7. He visits **Kuranes** in **Celephaïs**, sails to onyx **Inganok**, and is betrayed to **Leng** and its **high priest in the yellow mask**.
8. He leads **ghouls and night-gaunts** against the **Moon-beasts**.
9. He flies to **Kadath**, where **Nyarlathotep** tells him the sunset city is **his own childhood memory**, then tries to send him to **Azathoth**. Carter wakes.

**Keep this order and these places.** Change *why* things happen so the quest is heroic and lets the player be part of a war.

## 3. The new premise (pick one, or mix)

### A. The Gods Have Fled *(recommended, closest to the book)*
- **The setup:**
  - The **Great Ones**, the mild gods of earth, have abandoned Kadath and hidden in a sunset city that only one dreamer can see: the player.
  - Without them, **Nyarlathotep** has nothing to restrain him. His **Moon-beasts** and the **Men of Leng** sail out of the sky cities and attack the Dreamlands' coasts.
  - Nightmares start leaking into the dark-ages waking world as sickness, madness and failed harvests. That is the waking-world stake.
- **The quest:** find the sunset city, reach Kadath, and **persuade the gods to come home**, while the Dreamlands fight a war that grows act by act.
- **The twist (from the book):** the sunset city is **the player's own memory of Arkham at sunset**. It is the shop, the river, the gambrel roofs, all made marvellous by memory.

### B. The Siege of the Dreamlands
- The war comes first. The Moon-beasts' sky city, **fallen Serannian**, launches an invasion in the first act, and the cities of the book fall one by one unless the player helps.
- The dream-quest to Kadath is the **desperate plan** to end the war at its source.

### C. The Heir of Carter
- **Randolph Carter** made this journey a generation ago and **never came back**.
- The player follows his trail through his notes and his silver key, and meets everyone who remembers him.
- At Kadath, they learn what became of him: he took the gods' place in the sunset city, or he is Nyarlathotep's prisoner, or he is the Archsage (see §5).

## 4. Setting: the dark ages, both worlds

- **Waking world:** a walled **Arkham** on the Miskatonic in a dark age:
  - plague bells, a monastery college, a market square, the shop;
  - a **Collegium** with a chained library on the hill, which becomes Miskatonic in all but name.
- **The Dreamlands:** already medieval in feel (galleys, caravans, onyx temples, walled ports), so they fit the dark ages naturally. The **sky cities** are the one fantastical exception.
- **Time runs differently:** a night in Arkham is weeks in the Dreamlands. Waking up is a fast-travel and story device (§9).
- **One possible frame for the dark ages:** the Dreamlands look as they do because people in the waking world dream them. A dark age dreams a medieval Dreamlands.

## 5. The Archsage

**Where you meet him:** at the **Collegium library in Arkham**, in Act 1. Some options:
- The player's dreams are so vivid they become dangerous: sleepwalking, or waking up with sand from the Enchanted Wood in the bed. The shopkeeper, **Abner Pell**, sends the player to the Collegium for help.
- The player delivers an order of candles and ink to the library, finds a book that describes the sunset city exactly, and the Archsage is waiting at the reading desk.
- **Magister Henry Armitage** runs the library. The Archsage is a strange guest scholar who has been given a cell in its tower.

**What he teaches:** a **training montage at the Collegium**, in the style of Morrowind's guild quests:
- **Light and fire** (useful against ghasts).
- **Mindspeak**: telepathy, the Archsage's channel for the rest of the game.
- **Lucid dreaming**: changing small things in the Dreamlands.
- **Warding**: protecting yourself, not sealing gates.
- The **Dreamer's Waking**: an escape spell that sends you back to Arkham.

**What carries over from V1:**
- He is a **former high priest of Cthulhu from the Drowned Coast near R'lyeh**, spared long ago by an ancient warrior.
- He **never fights for you**. He speaks only in your mind (Mindspeak).
- He knows the enemy's black magic because he once used it.

**New hooks:**
- **The Archsage cannot enter the Dreamlands.** Cthulhu's sleep holds his dreams; if he dreamed, R'lyeh would find him. That is why he needs a dreamer, and why he only *speaks*.
- **He may be Atal's old pupil, or Atal's rival.** In the book, Atal climbed Hatheg-Kla with **Barzai the Wise**, who was taken by the Other Gods. One idea: the Archsage *was* Barzai, or the young priest Barzai was looking for.
- **Optional:** if premise C is chosen, the Archsage turns out to be **Carter, very old**.
- **Keep the V1 time loop?** The warrior who spared the young priest was the player, returning through dream-time at the end. It still works in V2 and gives the ending a strong twist. Or cut it to save complexity.

## 6. Act structure (following the book)

Each act has a **Dream-Quest beat** (the book), an **invasion beat** (the war), **RPG content** (towns, quests, levelling) and a **boss or set piece**.

| Act | Book beat | Invasion beat | Hub / RPG content | Boss / set piece |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Arkham** | Three dreams of the sunset city | Nightmare sickness in Arkham; refugees from "nowhere" | Shop work, the town, the **Collegium training**, bandits on the river road | A sleepwalking nightmare given flesh in the Collegium's cellar (tutorial boss) |
| **2. The Descent** | Seventy steps, the Cavern of Flame, Nasht and Kaman-Thah, seven hundred steps | The priests say the gate-guardians have seen **black sails above the clouds** | The **Enchanted Wood**: zoogs (tricky, can become allies), first Dreamlands loot | A zoog war-chief, or talking your way past the zoog council |
| **3. Ulthar and Dylath-Leen** | Atal; the merchant with the ruby; the black galley | **Dylath-Leen is occupied** by Men of Leng "merchants." The galleys are the invasion's vanguard | **Ulthar** (cats, temple, Atal) is the first proper town; Dylath-Leen has a thieves' quarter, smuggling and a resistance | Drugged onto a galley → **escape on the Moon** with the cats. Boss: the **Shantak Rider** from V1, now on the Moon's docks |
| **4. Oriab and Ngranek** | The carved face of the gods; night-gaunts | Oriab's harbour city **Baharna** is under **siege** from the sea; defend the walls | Baharna (a large port town), lava-field quests, a climbing ascent | The climb up Ngranek; captured by **night-gaunts** (a Sanity set piece) |
| **5. The Underworld** | The Vale of Pnath, the ghouls, Pickman, the gugs, the ghasts, the Tower of Koth | The ghouls are a **potential army**; the Moon-beasts hold some of them captive | The ghoul warrens become a faction town; ghast hunting; sneaking through the city of the gugs | Escape up the Tower of Koth with a gug pursuing; or a gug champion |
| **6. Celephaïs and Inganok** | Kuranes; the onyx city; the northern way | Kuranes' **Serannian has fallen** and become the enemy's sky city (from V1) | Celephaïs (royal court, knighthood quests), Inganok (quarries, onyx temple) | **Betrayed** in Inganok → carried to Leng |
| **7. Leng** | The monastery; the high priest in the yellow mask | The **source of the slave armies**; break their supply | A hostile wasteland of villages, prisoner rescue and stealth | **The high priest not to be described** (V1's Apprentice could be under the mask) |
| **8. The War in the Sky** | Carter leads the ghouls and night-gaunts against the Moon-beasts | **The big battle**: the player's allied factions attack **fallen Serannian** | Recruit whoever you befriended: cats, ghouls, night-gaunts, Celephaïs knights, zoogs | Storm the sky city; boss: a **Moon-beast admiral** or a corrupted cloud-galley |
| **9. Kadath** | Nyarlathotep's welcome; the truth about the sunset city; the Shantak to Azathoth | The war is won or lost here | — | **Nyarlathotep:** a refusal, an escape, or a fight (§10) |
| **Epilogue** | Carter wakes in Boston | The player wakes in Arkham **at sunset** | — | Reunion with Pell and the Archsage |

**Demo cut:** Acts 1–3. The demo ends with the **Shantak Rider** on the Moon and the cats' rescue. Ulthar, the Enchanted Wood and Arkham are the three hubs.

## 7. The invasion (traditional, no portals)

- **Fleets:** **Black galleys** sail off the edge of the world and back down from the sky, as in the book. They land at ports like any raiding fleet.
- **Sky cities:**
  - **Fallen Serannian:** Kuranes' cloud city, taken and blackened, now the main base.
  - **The Moon-beasts' towns on the Moon.**
  - Optional: **a third, moving city** pulled by Shantaks, the endgame fortress.
- **Shantak cavalry:** flying raiders that strike villages and caravans; they make the open world feel dangerous.
- **Men of Leng** are the infantry and the spies. Disguised as turbaned merchants, they're already inside the cities, buying slaves and rubies.
- **Occupation, not just raids:** some cities fall during the game.
  - Dylath-Leen is occupied from the start.
  - Baharna is under siege.
  - Hlanith or Thran could fall during Act 6 **if the player didn't help**.
- **Collaborators:** desperate townsfolk who traded with the galleys for rubies. Expose them or save them.
- **War-state system:** each region has **Free / Contested / Occupied** states. They change patrols, prices, merchants, the music and which quests are available. This replaces V1's Doom with something more concrete. Keep Doom as the global total.
- **Siege battles in Morrowind:**
  - Scripted waves.
  - Allied NPCs who can die.
  - Repairable gates.
  - Shantak strafing runs.
  - Braziers to light against ghasts.
- **The waking-world front:** no armies cross into Arkham, but the **nightmare sickness** spreads with the war. Arkham's state mirrors the Dreamlands: as the war goes badly, more people sleepwalk, and bells ring for the dead.

## 9. RPG and cosmic-horror systems that fit V2

- **Sleep is travel:** sleeping in the waking world sends you to the Dreamlands, and waking returns you to Arkham. Dying in the Dreamlands wakes you **with a Sanity cost** instead of a game over, a few times.
- **Dream-Signs** (V1 birthsigns) and **Callings** (V1 classes) still work.
- **Sanity and Insight** (V1): Insight is what lets you finally *see* the sunset city's streets in Act 9.
- **The Collegium as a guild:** a rank ladder, spell vendors, a library of readable books (Lovecraft excerpts plus new in-world texts).
- **Factions:**
  - Cats of Ulthar, Ghouls, the Collegium, Celephaïs' Knights, the Inganok quarrymen.
  - **Zoogs** as a risky alliance.
  - An evil path: the **Ruby Merchants**, who collaborate with the enemy.
- **Dream-Shapes** (V1): cat, ghoul and night-gaunt forms, earned from those factions.
- **Lucid tricks:** small "dream edits" with long cooldowns: open a locked door that "was never locked," make a bridge from a story you remember, call up a memory of Arkham as a safe zone.
- **Memories as currency:**
  - The sunset city is built from the player's memories, so side-quests in Arkham (helping people, keeping the shop going) add **Memories**.
  - Memories unlock districts of the sunset city in the finale.
  - Good deeds in the waking world matter in the dream.
- **Bandits and fantasy monsters:**
  - Caravan robbers on the Skai river road.
  - Wolves in Arkham's woods.
  - Goblin-like **Lurking Fear** degenerates in the hills.
  - Pirates off Oriab.
  - Bog-things near Kiran.
- **Loot with lore:**
  - The silver key.
  - Kuranes' signet.
  - A Moon-wine flask (restores Sanity, costs Insight).
  - Ghoul-forged bone blades.
  - Onyx armour from Inganok.
  - The merchant's ruby (cursed).

## 10. Endings

1. **The gods come home (heroic):** you refuse Nyarlathotep's Shantak, or survive it. The Great Ones return to Kadath, the invasion collapses, and you wake in Arkham at sunset, where everything looks like the city you dreamed.
2. **Carter's ending (bittersweet):** you learn the city was only home all along, and you wake. The war goes on without you, its outcome depending on the **War-state** of each region.
3. **The Crawling Chaos wins (dark):** you accept Nyarlathotep's offer and become his herald. The sky cities keep the Dreamlands.
4. **Optional Archsage twist:** the time loop from V1, or the reveal that the Archsage is Barzai or Carter, plays in the epilogue.

## 12. Quick side-quest ideas

- **Arkham:**
  - Pell's debts.
  - A candle-maker's ledger in a dream-script.
  - A child at the monastery who draws the sunset city.
- **Collegium:**
  - Retrieve a stolen book from a ghoul-haunted burying ground. The ghoul is friendly; it was trading, not stealing.
- **Enchanted Wood:**
  - Broker a truce between the zoogs and a lost cat, or betray the cat.
  - A zoog moon-tree with a ladder to the Moon (from the book): a secret early shortcut.
- **Ulthar:**
  - The law against killing cats (*The Cats of Ulthar*): a murder mystery where the suspect is a traveller who hates cats.
  - Atal's lost rope from Hatheg-Kla.
- **Dylath-Leen:**
  - Help the resistance smuggle children off before the galleys take them.
  - Steal the ruby ledger.
- **Baharna:**
  - Guard the lava-quarry.
  - A climber's lost party on Ngranek.
- **Underworld:**
  - Help a ghoul find Pickman's old painting.
  - Escort a ghoul through gug territory.
- **Celephaïs:**
  - A knighthood tournament.
  - A Kuranes memory-quest into his own dreams of Cornwall: a strange "dream inside a dream."
- **Inganok:**
  - The onyx quarry, where something is quarrying from the other side.
- **Hatheg-Kla (optional):**
  - Climb where Barzai vanished. An Archsage-reveal quest.

## 13. Smaller-scope checklist

- One exterior landmass: the Dreamlands, roughly following the book's map. Arkham is a small separate area (an exterior-like interior or a small island).
- About 8 towns: Arkham, Ulthar, Dylath-Leen, Baharna, Celephaïs, Inganok, a Leng village, the ghoul warrens. Plus the Moon, fallen Serannian and Kadath as special zones.
- About 10 enemy types:
  - Bandits, wolves, zoogs, Men of Leng, Moon-beasts, Shantaks, ghasts, gugs, night-gaunts, the Lurking-Fear degenerates.
  - All of them are in [CREATURES.md](CREATURES.md) and mostly use vanilla bases.
- New meshes needed: **cats** (essential), Shantak and Moon-beast parts, gug parts. All are Meshy candidates (THE_DREAMLANDS.md §16).
- Removed systems:
  - Portal sealing.
  - Waking-world region travel.
  - The Change (Innsmouth).
  - Mind-Exchange (Yith).
  - Shoggoth Mimicry.
  - The Hound's Curse.
  - These are post-game hooks or cut.
