XVI. Of Dreams

When Carter woke he knew that his sleep had been very vivid indeed. That he had dreamed of things almost impossible, and in such distant places and epochs as might easily lead back to outer realities if there were signs connecting him with the dreamland, could scarcely be denied; but still would men call his dreams mere fantasies, since he had known these dreams before, and recalled many a thing within them.
